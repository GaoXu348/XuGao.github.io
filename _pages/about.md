I'm Xu Gao, a graduate of Tsinghua University's Xingjian College, where I studied Theoretical and Applied Mechanics and Energy and Power Engineering as a double major. My background spans engineering, computational modeling, energy systems, computational materials science, and machine learning.

My current research explores how AI can support scientific investigation, from modeling and simulation to intelligent research tools and agent-assisted scientific computing. Recent projects include AgentLab, an extensible platform for scientific computing, and an artifact-grounded benchmark for autonomous DFT agent frameworks. More broadly, I'm interested in connecting AI, computational methods, and physical experimentation to address scientific problems across disciplines.

## Research Interests

* **AI for Scientific Discovery:** Exploring how artificial intelligence can support scientific understanding, reasoning, hypothesis development, and discovery across disciplines.
* **Scientific Machine Learning:** Applying data-driven and physics-aware modeling approaches to scientific problems and complex physical systems.
* **Computational & Experimental Science:** Integrating physical modeling, numerical simulation, scientific data, and experimental investigation to study real-world systems.
* **Intelligent Scientific Systems:** Developing and studying AI-enabled research tools, scientific agents, and computational systems that support complex scientific tasks.
